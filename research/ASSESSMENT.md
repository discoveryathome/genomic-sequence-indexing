# Current sequence-indexing assessment

Ready for limited pre-release contributor distribution and expert-agent review.
This is computational exact-string software research on public reference sequences,
with no wet-lab or biological-effect work.

Round two verified all sampled counts, added a persisted cache and an uncompressed
suffix-array control, and measured startup, throughput and whole-process memory.
Confirmation supports modest gains; shared-machine variability and workload
limits remain explicit. No novel method or production benefit is established.
